Know Your Rights & Legal Protections

Understanding essential laws can protect you in daily life. Here are key legal sections every citizen should know.

Important Laws You Should Know

Section 144 IPC

Prohibits the gathering of four or more people in public places to prevent riots.

Article 21 - Right to Life

Every citizen has the right to live with dignity and personal liberty.

Section 498A IPC

Protects women from domestic violence and cruelty in marriage.

Section 509 IPC

Deals with the punishment for sexual harassment and offensive gestures.

RTI Act, 2005

Gives citizens the right to request information from government authorities.

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Protects consumers from unfair trade practices and fraudulent products.

POSH Act, 2013

Law against sexual harassment at the workplace.

Section 66A IT Act

Deals with sending offensive messages online (struck down in 2015).
